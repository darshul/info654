About me

Hi! My name is Daria Shulga and welcome to my gallery! I'm a second semester MLIS student at Pratt Institute. I have a B.A. in Media Arts Production from Emerson College. I also graduated with a minor in Art History.

My gallery consists of a few artworks that I appreciate. On my free days (if I don't have tasks to do), I love to go to The MET and bring my notebook and a pen. When I do this, I go to a wing and choose a work to write about. It's a way to stimulate my brain and think critically about art (since I'm missing this aspect in the MLIS program). It's a very fun activity for me and I can spend hours selecting and writing. After I write it, I look at the curator's interpretation of said artwork. There's been moments where my interpretation parallels the curator's; but, most of the time, it's quite different. When it's different, I like to see how the curator interprets it and see if I still stand with my original interpretation or if it changes. Most of the time, I tweak it a bit.

This is my gallery of my favorite images from The MET's open access gallery. All of the rights to the images and information about said images goes to the museum.
